You Won't BELIEVE What This Small-Town Mayor Signed On Tuesday (Number 3 Will Shock You)

By Dana Williams

18 April 2026

When Mayor Patricia Alvarez walked into the council chambers of Glenwood Falls on Tuesday morning, no one could have predicted what would happen next. In a move that's already being called HISTORIC, she signed three separate documents — and you need to see what they were.

What she signed:

First, a routine revision to the municipal waste-collection schedule that shifts pickup from Thursday to Wednesday in the northern ward. Second, an updated zoning variance for a new dental office on Elm Street. And third — this is the one everyone is talking about — a proclamation declaring the third week of May as "Local Small Business Appreciation Week."

The proclamation has no budgetary impact and is largely ceremonial, a city clerk confirmed.

Mayor Alvarez declined to comment beyond a brief statement: "These are all normal parts of running a city. I signed four similar documents last month and no one wrote an article about it."

The documents were co-signed by the deputy mayor in accordance with standard municipal procedure. The next council meeting is scheduled for May 6.
