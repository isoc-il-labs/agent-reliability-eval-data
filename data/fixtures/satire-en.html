Local Man Who Has Never Cooked An Egg Named Head Chef At Presidential Residence

Staff Report

WASHINGTON — Sources confirmed Monday that Jerry Pemberton, a 47-year-old regional sales manager who has reportedly never once in his life cracked an egg into a pan, has been unanimously appointed as the new Head Chef at the Presidential Residence.

"We're thrilled to have someone with Jerry's complete lack of experience bringing a fresh perspective to the kitchen," said Chief of Staff Marcia Lin, adding that Pemberton's résumé consisted primarily of a 1997 driving-school certificate and a Little League participation ribbon.

Pemberton, reached for comment at the drive-through of a regional fast-food franchise, said he was "pretty pumped" about the new role and planned to spend his first day "figuring out which of the spoons are the big ones."

The appointment has already prompted cautious reactions from culinary authorities. "I've eaten three of the gentleman's attempts at toast," said James Beard-winning chef René Dubois. "I want to be very clear that what he produced was not food."

At press time, Pemberton was reportedly standing in the Residence's kitchen, staring thoughtfully at a carrot, trying to remember if you eat the orange part or the green part.
